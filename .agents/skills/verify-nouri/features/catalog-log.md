# Log a catalog food

Choose `2026-09-25` with the `Choose log date` picker. Press `Ctrl+K` (or choose `+ Log` in the sidebar) and choose `Log food` (see [command-palette.md](command-palette.md)). Type `apple` in the search. Once typing pauses, the `Foods` group lists `Apple, flesh and skin, raw`, highlighted first. Press Enter. The amount step opens with `#palette-amount` at `100` g; fill it with `150` (the preview changes to start with `81 kcal`), capture a screenshot, and press Enter (`Log 1 food · 81 kcal`). The palette closes, the timeline shows the apple at 150 g and 81 kcal, and the summary increases by 81 kcal. Save an authenticated `GET /v1/days/2026-09-25` response and check its catalog reference and nutrients.

The time picker (`#palette-time`, a popover trigger) sits in the food search's footer and in the amount step, and resets to the current local time each time the palette opens. Click it, then `button[aria-label="Hour 08"]` and `button[aria-label="Minute 15"]`, and press Escape (this closes only the popover). The trigger reads `08:15`; after saving, the stored `occurredAt` is 08:15 local time on the chosen date. Typing works too: with the popover open, type `0815` and it sets 08:15 and closes; `12` alone sets the hour, and Enter closes. The list scrolls with the mouse wheel and touch.
