# Recent foods

Choose `2026-09-25` with the `Choose log date` picker and log the catalog apple at 150 g (see [catalog-log.md](catalog-log.md)), then the manual foods `Verification oats` (80 g, 311 kcal) and, later in the day, `verification OATS` (60 g, 233 kcal, protein 10, carbs 50, fat 5) through `Add a food manually`. Before anything is logged, the palette's food step has no `Recent` group.

Choose `Next day` twice to reach `2026-09-27`, then press `Ctrl+K` and choose `Log food`. The `Recent` group shows each food once, most recently eaten first, filtered by what is typed. The two oats entries appear as a single `verification OATS` row at 60 g, the latest one, with its amount and kcal. Press Enter on it. `#palette-amount` reads `60`; fill it with `40`, and the preview changes to `155 kcal · P 6.7 · C 33.3 · F 3.3`. Set `#palette-time` to 12:30 and press Enter. The page sends `POST /v1/food-entries/batches` with `{"entryId":"<latest oats id>","amount":40}`. `GET /v1/days/2026-09-27` shows the oats at 40 g, 155.333 kcal, and `occurredAt` 12:30 local time.

Several at once: highlight `verification OATS` and press `Shift+Enter`, then the apple, then search `banana` and `Shift+Enter` a result (or click each row's tick box, `Tick <name>`). Ticks survive new searches. Clear the search: the first item is `Log 3 foods` with their names and total kcal. `Shift+Enter` on a ticked row unticks it; in an amount step, an `Also logging:` chip's `Remove <name>` drops it. Enter on `Log 3 foods`: one batch request logs all three at the palette's time, in the order ticked, and a failing item logs none of them.

Logging the apple from `Recent` at 200 g records 108 kcal and keeps its Ciqual `catalogRef`. The server scales the recorded snapshot and never re-reads the catalog. Log again on a timeline row still lands today at the original amount and sends no `amount`.
