# Overview

Choose the `Overview` sidebar link and check the `Overview categories` tabs: All, Activity, Measurements, and Sleep. Activity holds the `Workouts` tile next to `Steps`, then `Active energy` and `Heart rate`. Food, water, and supplements live only on My Day. On a fresh account with no earlier records, each section shows dashes and empty states, Activity reads `No steps imported for this day.` and `No active energy imported for this day.` (it fills only from [wearable import](wearables.md)), and Sleep shows a single `Time asleep` tile reading `No sleep logged for this day.`

Measurements carry forward from earlier dates, and Progress photos shows up to 12 photos on or before the selected date.

The only log control inside the sections is the Progress photos tile's `Upload photo` button, which opens the file picker for the photo input below. Every other record starts from `+ Log` in the sidebar. Press `Ctrl+K`, choose `Log measurement`, then `Height`, and save `180` in `#palette-measurement`; then type `weight 81.5` at the root and press Enter on the quick log. The Weight tile reads `81.5 kg` and the BMI tile reads `25.2`, labeled Overweight. Press `Ctrl+K`, choose `Start workout`, then `Log a past activity`, and save a 45-minute Run with `8.2` km and `520` kcal. The Workouts tile reads `45 min`, with `1 logged · 520 kcal · 8.2 km` beside its title. Press `Ctrl+K` and choose `Log sleep`, with the default 23:00 and 07:00 times, and enter Light 230, Deep 90, REM 110, and Awake 20. Time asleep reads `8 h 0 min` and shows the stage bar with four labels above the session, in the same tile.

In `Logged this day`, choose `Edit weight 81.5 kg`. The `Edit weight` dialog shows `#edit-measurement-value` at 81.5 and the logged time. Enter `80.9` and choose `Save changes`. `Measurement updated.` appears, the dialog closes, the row and the Weight tile read `80.9 kg`, and in `GET /v1/overview/<today>` the entry keeps its id with `revision` 2. Edit it back to `81.5` the same way so the values below hold.

Press `Ctrl+K`, choose `Start workout`, then `Log a past activity`, and switch Activity to `Strength training`. Distance disappears. Enter a duration, then choose `Add exercise` and pick `Barbell Squat` in the picker, with 3 sets, 8 reps per set, and 60 kg. Add `Pushups` with 3 sets and 12 reps, leaving weight empty. Save and check both exercises in the Activity tab's workout row and `GET /v1/overview/<today>` under `sports.entries[].strengthExercises`; `distanceM` must be `null`.

Set a small PNG on the hidden `Choose a progress photo` input. A thumbnail named `Open photo from <date>` appears. Opening it shows the image in a dialog. Close the viewer, then move to the next day and confirm the measurements and photo remain while the daily workout and sleep lists are empty. Return to the original day before the readbacks and deletion below.

Read `GET /v1/overview/<today>` and assert these values: `sports.entryCount` 2, the run has `distanceM` 8200, the strength workout has `distanceM: null` and two exercises, `measurements.bmi` is 25.2, `sleep.totalMinutes` is 480 with stages, and one `photos` item has `contentType: image/png`. Delete the run through `Delete run at …`, and the photo through `Delete photo` inside the dialog its thumbnail opens. Read the overview again and confirm that `sports.entryCount` is 1, the strength workout remains, and `photos` is empty. Delete the strength workout through `Delete strength training at …` and confirm `sports.entryCount` is 0. The `Sleep` tab shows only the Sleep section, with its `Time asleep` tile.
