# Targets

Choose `Targets` in the sidebar under SETTINGS. The dialog shows the defaults: `Energy (kcal)` 2000, `Water (ml)` 2000, `Sleep (h)` 8, and an empty `Goal weight (kg)`. Fill energy 1800, water 2500, sleep 7.5, and goal weight 72.5, then choose `Save targets`. On `/`, the Energy gauge reads `Energy · 1,800` and the water card reads `2,500 ml goal`. On `/overview`, Sleep shows `7 h 30 min goal`, and Weight shows `Goal 72.5 kg`.

Targets apply from today. Choose `Previous day`: the Overview returns to `8 h 0 min goal` with no weight goal. Read `GET /v1/targets/<today>` (`effectiveFrom` is today) and `GET /v1/targets/<yesterday>` (`effectiveFrom` is `null`, default targets).

A failed targets read hides only the comparisons. Intercept `GET /v1/targets/<date>` before an uncached page load (for example, route it to 503 and reload). A failed background refetch of a cached target does not show this error state. `/overview` still shows every section with no target bars, and `Targets could not load: …` with `Try again` appears once, above the sections. On `/`, the message takes the cards' place; the timeline stays. Once the endpoint recovers, `Try again` brings back the cards, with `Energy · 1,800` on the gauge after the save above.

## Suggest from profile

The dialog opens with a `SUGGEST FROM PROFILE` section: `Sex` (`#profile-sex`), `Age (years)`, `Activity` (`#profile-activity`), `Goal` (`#profile-goal`: `Lose weight`, `Maintain weight`, `Gain weight`), and `Suggest targets`. Without a weight or height it reads `Log your weight and height on Overview to get a suggestion.` After logging weight 68 and height 168 (Overview or `POST /v1/measurements`), reopen the dialog, enter age 34, keep `Female` and `Moderate`, and choose `Lose weight`. `Suggest targets` fills `Energy (kcal)` 1730, `Protein (g)` 136, `Carbs (g)` 166, `Fat (g)` 58, and `Water (ml)` 2400, leaves the other fields alone, and shows `Filled in energy, macros, and water for 68 kg, 168 cm, age 34. Maintenance is about 2,170 kcal. Check them, then save.` Capture `GET /v1/targets/<today>` before suggesting and compare it afterwards: suggesting leaves the saved targets unchanged until `Save targets`. On a fresh account `effectiveFrom` stays `null`; after the save in the first recipe, it stays today with the saved values. `GET /v1/profile/<today>` returns the profile with `birthYear` equal to the current year minus 34, the same suggestion, and `missing: []`. Reopening the dialog shows age 34 and `Lose weight`.
