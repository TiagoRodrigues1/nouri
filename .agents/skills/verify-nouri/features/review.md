# Review

Seed a week through the API: two foods on one day, a food without protein on another, a night that ends on a third day, a weight on a fourth, and a workout on a fifth distinct day. Choose the `Review` sidebar link. The heading reads `Last 7 days`, the `7-day review` table lists the seven days oldest first, and the line above it reads `Records on 5 of 7 days`. Days with no record of a kind have empty cells, not zeros; each holds visually hidden `Not logged` text, which snapshots and `innerText` include. The day whose food lacks protein shows `Incomplete` under Protein. The night shows on the day it ends. The `Days recorded` footer counts days per column.

Choose `Open <day> in Today`. Its link contains `?date=<day>`, and Today opens on that date with its foods, including after reload. Press Back and check that the same week is shown. Choose `Open <day> in Overview` for the weighed day; its link also contains `?date=<day>`, and the Weight tile shows the value after navigation or reload. Return to Review and choose `Previous range`. The URL gains `?to=<date>`, the heading shows the range, and the rows move back seven days. Open `Choose the last day of the range`: the calendar opens on the month containing the selected last day, and the days of the range are shaded. Choosing another day moves the range to end there. Opening a day from there and pressing Back returns to that week.

Read `GET /v1/review/<date>` and assert `from`, the seven `days`, the null metrics on unrecorded days, and `recordedDays`.

## Range and weight chart

The `Review range` select beside the arrows offers `7 days`, `2 weeks`, `1 month`, and `3 months`. Seed weights across two months through the API, then choose `3 months`: the URL gains `?days=90`, the heading reads `Last 90 days`, the table has 90 rows, and `Previous range` moves back 90 days. The `Weight` card plots one dot per weighed day across the whole range, with the change from the first to the last weigh-in (`−2.9 kg since Sun, Aug 16`, Unicode minus). A range with no weights shows `No weight logged in these days.` `GET /v1/review/<date>?days=30` returns 30 days; values outside `7`, `14`, `30`, and `90` answer 400.

From an earlier Review window, press `Ctrl+K` and choose `Log food`. The footer must read `Logging to <window's last day>`. Log a manual food and read `GET /v1/days/<window-end>` to confirm it landed there, then compare that day's calories and protein with `GET /v1/review/<window-end>`.

## Targets comparison

Save targets taking effect before the window (for example 1,800 kcal and 120 g protein) and a change from the window's fourth day (2,200 kcal, 100 g). The Targets dialog only saves today's targets, so use `PUT /v1/targets/<date>` with the full targets object from `GET /v1/targets/<date>` and the two values changed. Log foods on three days with calories and protein, one close to its target and one far off, and a food without calories on a fourth day. Open that week in Review. The line above the table reads `Complete food records on 3 of 7 days`. The `Calories` and `Protein` cards show the average over the three days, the mean target, and `Within 10% of target on N of 3` or `Reached target on N of 3`. Each complete day shows its signed difference from that day's target under the total (`+50`, `−800` with a Unicode minus, or `on target` when equal); the day with the missing calorie value shows `Incomplete` and no difference. A week with no complete days shows `No complete food days to compare yet.` in both cards. Read `GET /v1/review/<date>` and assert each day's `targets` switch on the change date and `nutrition` matches the cards.

## Water, supplements, weekly trends, and suggestions

Save a two-item supplement list through `PUT /v1/supplement-stack/<date>` dated before the range. Then log water, sleep, foods, and supplement intakes over 14 days through the API: make the last week fall short (for example 1,100 ml of water, 6 h 40 min nights, protein under target, one supplement of two) and the week before it on target. Open `/review`. The table has `Water` (`1,100 ml`, blank where none) and `Supplements` (`1 of 2 taken`) columns, and the footer counts both. The `Water` and `Sleep` cards sit beside `Calories` and `Protein`: `Reached target on 0 of 5 days` and `0 of 7 nights`. The summary line ends with `Every supplement on your list taken on N of 7 days.` The `Suggestions` card names the last week (`From Sun, Oct 11 to Sat, Oct 17`) and lists each suggestion with its evidence, for example `Water reached target on 0 of 5 days, averaging 1,100 ml against 2,000 ml.` A week with fewer than four complete food days suggests only `Log every food for a few more days` for food. `Weekly trends` reads `Choose 2 weeks or longer to compare weeks.` at 7 days. At `?days=30` the `Weekly trends` table has four rows labelled `Sun, Sep 20 – Sat, Sep 26` and so on, with each week's daily averages, blank where a week has no records, and the change from the week before underneath (`−1,100 ml`). Read `GET /v1/review/<date>` and assert `weeks`, `water`, `sleep`, `supplements`, and `suggestions` match the page.
