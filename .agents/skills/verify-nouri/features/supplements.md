# Supplements

Stay on today's date. The `section[aria-label="Supplements"]` card on My Day should say `No supplements on your list for this day.`

Open the sidebar `Supplements` button (the card's `Add supplements` or `Edit your supplements` opens the same `Your supplements` dialog). In the dialog, choose `Add supplement` twice. Every row's fields share the accessible names `Name`, `Type`, `Dose`, and `Dose unit` (the unit's visible label reads `Unit`), so pick them by row index within the dialog, and match `Dose` exactly so it skips `Dose unit`. Fill the first row as `Vitamin D3`, dose `2000`, and the `Dose unit` combobox `IU`. Fill the second row as `Creatine monohydrate`, the `Type` combobox `Creatine`, dose `5`, and unit `g`. Choose `Save supplements`. The dialog closes and the card lists both rows, with `2,000 IU · Vitamin` and `5 g · Creatine`.

Choose `Mark Creatine monohydrate as taken` (its tick button). The card should read `1 of 2 taken` and the button changes to show the time. Read `GET /v1/days/<today>` and assert that `supplements.items` has two entries and that creatine has one intake with dose `{ amount: 5, unit: "g" }`. Choose `Unmark Creatine monohydrate, taken at <time>`: the tick button returns, the card reads `0 of 2 taken` and the day readback has no intakes. Choose `Previous day`. The card shows the empty state, because the list takes effect from the day it was saved.

Schedules: open the dialog again. Each row has a `Days to take <name>` toolbar of `Mon` to `Sun` toggle buttons, all pressed by default, and a `Times a day` combobox (`Once a day`). Set creatine's `Times a day` to `2× a day`, and unpress every day except one that is not today for Vitamin D3. Save. The card reads `0 of 1 taken` with Vitamin D3 muted, whose meta reads `2,000 IU · not due · <day>`. Tick creatine: its chip reads `1 of 2 · <time>`, a `Mark another Creatine monohydrate as taken` tick stays beside it, and the card still reads `0 of 1 taken`. Tick it again: the second tick goes away and the card reads `1 of 1 taken`. `GET /v1/days/<today>` lists creatine with `due: 2` and Vitamin D3 with `due: 0`. Ticking Vitamin D3 on its off day still logs it and leaves the count alone.

Overview has no Supplements section; ticking happens only on My Day.
