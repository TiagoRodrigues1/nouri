# Log a manual food

Choose `2026-09-25` with the `Choose log date` picker. Choose `+ Log`, then `Food` and the `Add manually` tab. The form takes the label per 100 g (or 100 ml from `Label is per`) and the amount eaten. Fill `#food-name` with `Verification oats`, `#manual-kcal` with `388.75`, `#manual-protein` with `16.25`, `#manual-carbs` with `66.25`, `#manual-fat` with `6.25`, and `#manual-amount` with `80`; leave `Share with other Nouri users` unticked. Capture a screenshot, then press Enter in `#manual-amount`. The entry stores 80 g with 311 kcal, 13 g protein, 53 g carbs, 5 g fat and `catalogRef: null`. Check the `Logged foods` row and summary, reload to check persistence, and read `GET /v1/days/2026-09-25` to confirm the stored amount and nutrients.
