# Browse dates and delete

Create an entry on `2026-09-25`. Open the `Choose log date` picker and choose `Thursday, September 24th, 2026` to check the prior day; the popover closes and the button reads `Sep 24, 2026`. Off today the `h1` shows the full date, such as `Thursday, September 24, 2026`. The popover's `Today` button returns to the current date. Use the `Previous day` and `Next day` buttons to navigate; the selected date and entries must change together. Return to `2026-09-25`, capture the food row, then choose its `Delete <food name>` button. It deletes at once, with no confirmation, and toasts `Food removed from your log.` The row disappears and the summary above the timeline updates (`<n> foods · <kcal> kcal`, or `0 foods · — kcal` once the day is empty). Changing the date dismisses toasts, so capture the toast first. Read `GET /v1/days/2026-09-25` before and after deletion to confirm persistence.
